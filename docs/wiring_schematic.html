⚡ Electrical Rules & Warnings

  • All logic is 3.3V: Never apply 5V directly to nRF52840 or R558S pins.
  • PN532 SPI Mode: Ensure DIP switches are both at 0 (OFF).
  • R558S 3.3VT: Must be powered continuously (3.3V) for capacitive touch detection to wake MCU.
  • NFC Shielding: Keep metal and LiPo cells separated from the PN532 antenna using a 13.56MHz ferrite absorber sheet.
